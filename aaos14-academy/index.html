The 30-second mental model

AAOS in one sentence

AAOS is full Android running on the car's computer, plus CarService (the policy brain) and the VHAL (the hardware interpreter). Apps never talk to the CAN bus directly — every request passes through a chain of control:

App → CarPropertyManager ⇢ CarService ⇢ IVehicle (AIDL) → VHAL → MCU / CAN → ECU

⇢ = Binder IPC (crosses a process boundary) · → = direct call / bus transmission

🏨
The big-picture analogy

Picture the car as a hotel. Apps are guests; CarService is the front desk checking permissions (“does your key card open this floor?”); the VHAL is the maintenance technician who speaks the “language” of the boiler, elevators and air conditioning; the ECUs are those machines.

Guests never walk into the plant room to adjust the boiler — they call the front desk.
